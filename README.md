## Building

Products and experiments at different stages of development. Status labels distinguish public launches from demonstrations, prototypes and research concepts.

- **[WhoGoWin](https://whogowin.app)** · **Early-stage launch** — Mobile-first social prediction platform exploring what Africa thinks will happen next.

- **[NoBed.ai](https://github.com/macjordan09/nobed-ai)** · **Demo MVP** — Ghana-focused hospital capacity intelligence and emergency referral coordination. Demonstration data only; not live clinical availability.

- **[DVLA Diaspora Connect](https://github.com/macjordan09/DVLA-Diaspora-Connect)** · **Proof-of-concept MVP** — Exploring digital access to Ghanaian driver and vehicle licensing services for the diaspora; not an official DVLA service.

- **[Eʋe App](https://github.com/macjordan09/ewe-app)** · **In development** — Interactive Eʋegbe language learning and cultural education, including lessons, pronunciation and proverbs.

- **Carry4Me / Fetch4Me** · **MVP exploration** — Community-led, identity-verified parcel delivery and matching between Ghana and the diaspora, with compliance and security gates.

- **Yelly** · **Early prototype** — Peer-to-peer car-sharing and community mobility for Northern Canadian communities.

- **Ghana Music Ledger** · **Research concept** — Exploring music usage reporting, licensing and transparent royalty accounting for Ghana's music ecosystem.
